# Google Ads Implementation Guide: WordPressRecovery.in

Because I cannot log into your Google Ads account, I have prepared this exact, click-by-click guide for you to create the campaign. 

It takes about 3 minutes to complete. Please open your Google Ads dashboard and follow these steps.

## Phase 1: Campaign Setup
1. In Google Ads, click the blue **+ Create** button and select **Campaign**.
2. **Objective**: Select **Leads**.
3. **Conversion Goals**: Ensure only **"WordPress Recovery Lead (IN)"** (the one you imported earlier) is selected. Click Continue.
4. **Campaign Type**: Select **Search**.
5. **Campaign Name**: `IN_Search_WP_Recovery_Brand`
6. Click **Continue**.

## Phase 2: Bidding & Settings
1. **Bidding**: Select **Clicks**.
2. Check the box for **"Set a maximum cost per click bid limit"**.
3. Enter **`150`**. Click Next.
4. **Networks**: **Uncheck** "Display Network". (Keep Search Network checked).
5. **Locations**: Select **India**. 
6. **Languages**: Ensure **English** is selected. Click Next.

## Phase 3: Ad Group & Keywords
1. **Ad Group Name**: `Emergency_Recovery`
2. **Keywords (Copy and paste exactly as shown, with the quotes):**

1. **Budget**: Select **Set custom budget** and enter **`600`**.
2. Click **Next**, review the campaign, and click **Publish Campaign**.
